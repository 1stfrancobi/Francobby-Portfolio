ABOUT ME

Making data useful, from observations to explanations.

I’m Francis, a geomatic engineer working across data analysis, GIS and applied machine-learning research. I use Python, Excel and spatial tools to organize information, examine patterns and explain findings clearly.

My work combines satellite-gravity modelling with GIS reporting and field-data verification in mining and construction. I approach analytical problems by checking the inputs, assessing the results and communicating their practical meaning and limitations.
